◆ network / privacy

Privacy

The short version: this is a game at DevFest Ilorin 2026. It keeps very little, and it all gets deleted after the event.

What's public

If you choose Approve & Merge, the leaderboard shows your name, your X or LinkedIn handle (linked to your profile), your commit message, your best score and how many games you played. Nothing else is shown to anyone.

What's stored

  • The name, handle and commit message you typed.
  • Your game results: score, bugs squashed, taps, and when you played.
  • A random ID stored on your phone (a cookie and local storage) so the game remembers you. It isn't linked to anything else.

If you play without merging, only the game results and that random ID are stored.

We don't use analytics, ads or trackers, and the server doesn't log IP addresses.

Why

To run the leaderboard, and so the organiser can connect with players afterwards using the handle you chose to share.

How long

Everything is deleted 60 days after the event.

Delete it now

Open the game on the same phone, go to Checks → Settings → Delete my data and tap it twice. Your name, handle, commit and all your games are removed immediately. Or message the organiser and they'll do it for you.
